# What is UHired?
UHired? is an AI-powered resume analysis platform that evaluates resumes against job descriptions and provides personalized feedback to improve ATS compatibility and interview readiness.

# How it Works
1. Create an Account — Sign up for a UHired? account and log in to access the resume analysis dashboard.

2. Upload Your Resume — Upload your resume as a PDF to begin the analysis process.

3. Provide a Job Description — Paste the job description for the position you are applying for so UHired? can evaluate how well your resume aligns with the role.

4. Analyze Resume Content — UHired? examines your resume for relevant keywords, skills, and technologies mentioned in the job description, identifying potential gaps between your qualifications and the job requirements.

5. Receive Personalized Feedback — Review suggestions for improving your resume's keyword relevance, ATS compatibility, and alignment with the target position.

6. Improve Interview Readiness — Use the analysis and recommendations to strengthen your resume, highlight relevant experience, and better prepare for job and internship applications.

The ultimate goal of UHired? is to help job seekers and students understand how their resumes align with specific job opportunities and make informed improvements before applying.
